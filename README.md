One word for my Wi-Fi: **Friendzoned**.

The router lets me in, shows full bars, remembers my name — and then loads absolutely nothing. We are connected. We are not together. It keeps me around for the company and gives me nothing back, which is exactly what being friendzoned is, except the friend is a small box with blinking lights that I have to unplug and plug back in before it will even look at me.

The entry is one word, 11 characters, written by `main.py` to `entry.txt` for the make.fun "One Word for Your Wi-Fi" bounty.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes `Friendzoned` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt` when that is unset), with no trailing newline and no whitespace.
